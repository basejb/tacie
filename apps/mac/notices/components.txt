================================================================================
3D-Speaker: ERes2Net and CAM++  (Alibaba, modelscope/3D-Speaker)
https://github.com/modelscope/3D-Speaker
Two speaker-embedding models, downloaded on first launch, tell a meeting's speakers apart and know remembered
attendees: ERes2Net base (iic/speech_eres2net_base_sv_zh-cn_3dspeaker_16k) and CAM++
(iic/speech_campplus_sv_zh_en_16k-common_advanced), the latter further trained by Tacie.
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
================================================================================

The CAM++ model was further trained on:

- AMI Meeting Corpus (University of Edinburgh and the AMI Consortium), https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Zeroth-Korean (Lucas Jo and Wonkyum Lee, Atlas Guide and Gridspace), https://www.openslr.org/40/,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the weights were fine-tuned for speaker grouping; the data itself does not ship with Tacie.
